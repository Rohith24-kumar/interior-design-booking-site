Our Story

Crafting Spaces That Inspire

Since 2010, we've been transforming ordinary spaces into extraordinary experiences through thoughtful Scandinavian design principles.

Our Journey

Founded in Stockholm in 2010, Nordic Designs began as a small studio with a big vision: to bring the tranquility and functionality of Scandinavian design to homes and businesses worldwide. What started with three passionate designers has grown into an award-winning studio with over 30 talented professionals and projects spanning 10+ countries.

Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that great design should enhance everyday life. We draw inspiration from the Nordic landscape—its clean lines, natural materials, and harmonious balance between form and function. Every project we undertake reflects our commitment to creating spaces that are not only beautiful but also deeply personal and practical.

Today, we continue to push boundaries while staying true to our core values of sustainability, craftsmanship, and client collaboration. Each space we design tells a unique story, and we're honored to be part of that narrative.
