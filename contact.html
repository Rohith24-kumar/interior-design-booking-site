FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and process.

How much does a design project cost?

+

Project costs vary based on scope, size, and complexity. Our residential projects typically start from ₹1,00,000, while commercial projects start from ₹3,00,000. We provide detailed quotes after understanding your specific requirements during a consultation.

How long does a typical project take?

+

Timeline depends on the project scope. Small residential projects can take 4-6 weeks, while larger homes or commercial spaces may take 3-6 months. We provide a detailed timeline during the planning phase and keep you updated throughout the process.

Do you work with clients outside Sweden?

+

Yes! We work with clients globally. We've completed projects in over 10 countries. For remote projects, we use video calls, 3D renderings, and detailed documentation to ensure seamless collaboration regardless of location.

What is included in your design services?

+

Our services include initial consultation, concept development, 3D renderings, material selection, furniture sourcing, project management, and installation oversight. We can also handle contractor coordination and custom furniture design for full-service projects.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

+

Yes, we offer virtual consultations via video call for clients who prefer remote meetings or are located outside Stockholm. We can discuss your project, review plans, and provide design recommendations virtually.

How do I get started with a project?

+

Simply fill out our contact form or call us to schedule an initial consultation. During this meeting, we'll discuss your vision, needs, and budget. We'll then provide a proposal outlining the scope, timeline, and investment required for your project.
